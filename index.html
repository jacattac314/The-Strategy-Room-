Our story

Built to fill a gap. Built to help.

Founded in 2023 with a simple conviction: that families and organizations both deserve access to expert, compassionate support — when and where they need it most.

Who we are

The Strategy Room brings compassionate and timely case management, behavioral health interventions, and consulting services to organizations and families of all walks of life. We are a small, experienced, deeply committed team based in St. Louis, Missouri.

"Whether you're a family in a time of crisis, or a behavioral health organization in need of process improvement or market share growth, The Strategy Room acts as the arm extension of any need you may have."

Why we exist

There is a gap in how care is accessed and delivered — both for families and for the organizations trying to serve them. Families navigating terminal illness, unexpected hospital stays, addiction crises, or rare diseases are left to manage impossibly complex systems, often alone.

At the same time, behavioral health organizations are stretched thin, needing expert support they cannot afford to hire permanently. We built The Strategy Room to address both sides of that gap.

We fly in, make our mark, and then leave you with a sense of peace and ease. No unnecessary entanglement. Just results.

Our mission

To bring clarity, capability, and compassion to moments that demand the most — empowering families to navigate healthcare with confidence, and helping behavioral health organizations build stronger, more impactful practices.
