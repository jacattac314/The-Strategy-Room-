Nature of Services

The Strategy Room LLC ("The Strategy Room," "we," "us," or "our") provides professional consulting, case management coordination, and advocacy services for families navigating complex healthcare situations and for behavioral health organizations seeking operational or strategic support.

Our services are advisory and coordinative in nature. We are not:

  • A licensed clinical practice or therapy provider
  • A licensed hospital, medical facility, or healthcare provider
  • A crisis hotline or emergency intervention service
  • A licensed social work agency
  • A law firm or legal service provider
  • A licensed insurance broker or benefits advisor

Not a Substitute for Professional Care

Nothing provided by The Strategy Room — including consultations, case management coordination, written materials, or guidance — constitutes or should be construed as medical advice, clinical treatment, legal advice, or financial advice.

Our work is intended to supplement, not replace, the care provided by licensed medical professionals, therapists, attorneys, and other credentialed specialists. We strongly encourage all clients to maintain relationships with appropriate licensed professionals for their specific needs.
